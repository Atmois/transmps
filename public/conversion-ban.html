Help End Conversion Therapy in the UK

As defined by the UN (source), "Conversion therapy is an umbrella term used to describe interventions of a wide-ranging nature, all of which have in common the belief that a person's sexual orientation or gender identity can and should be changed. Such practices [...] claim to aim at changing people from gay, lesbian or bisexual to heterosexual and from trans or gender diverse to cisgender." These practices are undeniably harmful to the victims and have zero evidence backing them in any form or context.

The UK Government (June 2026) released a Draft Bill to ban on conversion practices in England and Wales which is open for scrutiny and feedback, it is one of the best bills proposed however it still contains loopholes which can be abused to continue conversion practices on vulnerable people. We need to continue to put pressure on our MP's to not only ensure the bill keeps its strong protections but also to make sure the loopholes that remain are closed for good (see the example email for details):

As per Gendered Intelligence, the current draft bill includes 3 major issues within its text:

  • Healthcare Providers Loophole - The draft bill includes the wording that could enable conversion practitioners to operate under the guise of "health services". This loophole should be closed to ensure that open conversations can be had without allowing for abuse from professionals.
  • Supression Loophole - The draft bill does not include any protections against practices that seeks to "suppress” a person's sexual orientation or gender identity. The law should reflect that and ensure any perpetrators who practices any form of conversion therapy will be punished.
  • Threshold for Harm - The draft bill applies to "abusive conversion practices", implying the existence of non-abusive practices. All conversion practices are abuse, and the law should treat it as such.

Currently, a Draft Bill (https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/draft-conversion-practices-bill) to ban conversion practices in England and Wales is going through the process of pre-legislative scrutiny in Parliament. The bill was orignally proposed over 8 years ago and has not progressed after 6 successive Prime Ministers.

The Conservative Party, The Liberal Democrats, Greens, Plaid Cymru and SNP under successive leaders, alongside the current Labour Government have all made manifesto promises to deliver this vital protection for LGBTQ+ people, with the current Government's manifesto promising to deliver a "full trans-inclusive ban on conversion practices".

Conversion practices are any activity that intends to “change or suppress” a person's sexual orientation or gender identity, and any form of it causes significant long-term harm to its victims. It is vital that the bill bans all forms of conversion practice with no loopholes allowing the abuse to continue.

Firstly, the draft bill currently does not include wording to protect suppressing a person's sexual orientation or gender identity as a form of abuse under its protections. This allows for practitioners to shield themselves from the law by allowing them an exception if they do not "change" but only "supress" the victims sexual orientation or gender identity.
The draft bill also implies the existence of non-abusive conversion practices, which leaves the door open for so called "legal" forms of conversion therapy to be exercised and continue to harm victims. All conversion practices are abuse, and the law should treat it as such.
Finally, the bill leaves a loophole in the wording of Section 1(3) where it states that the conduct will only be counted as conversion therapy if "the person acts in a way that falls far below the standards reasonably expected of a person in their position". This could be used to allow medical professionals to continue conversion practices by not protecting victims from all forms of abuse if it is not dmeed to be below the aformentioned "standards".
