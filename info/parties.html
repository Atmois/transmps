Party Stances

This filter sorts the parties by their party policy on trans rights. If a party is listed as supportive, it means they have an official stance in support of trans rights. If a party is listed as unsupportive, it means they have an official stance against trans rights. If a party is not listed, it means they have no official stance on trans rights. New or smaller parties will likely not be listed for a while as they may not have a stance yet.

Please note that this will not necessarily reflect the views of individual MPs, as they may have different stances on trans rights than their party's official policy. This may also be out of date as parties may change their policies over time, so it is always a good idea to check the party's official website for the most up-to-date information.

Due to the nature of Independent MPs, they will not be listed here as they do not have a party stance, their info will come along with the individual MP breakdown. Sinn Féin MPs and the Speaker will not be listed as they do not hold any political stances within the House of Commons, their opinions however may vary.

We are working on an individual MP breakdown, however due to enormous amounts of information out there, it is taking us a while to compile it all and ensure it is accurate and up-to-date. If you see a mistake or have any missing information (or even more sources to add to the list), please contact us so we can fix it!
